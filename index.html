Quick reference

  • Walking: slow 18, normal 24, fast 30 miles per day before modifiers.
  • Horseback: about 30–40 miles on a good road; reduce for difficult routes and poor weather.
  • Boat: often 30–60 miles per day in ordinary sailing conditions; favorable wind can increase this.
  • Spelljammer: use journey-time guidance for destinations. This calculator's 100-million-mile/day scale is a homebrew placeholder, not an official universal speed.

These are homebrew estimates, not official rules. Actual progress can be affected by rests, getting lost, hazards, vessel type, currents, crew, and encounter delays. Tailwind and headwind modifiers primarily apply to boats.

DM tip: use the estimate as a starting point, then let interesting events—not constant rolls—shape the journey.
