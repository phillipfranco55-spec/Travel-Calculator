DM tip: Weather is a storytelling tool first. Apply travel penalties when they make the journey more interesting, not to punish the party for every rainy day.

d20 Weather Table

d20WeatherSuggested effect
1Violent stormExposed travel may be dangerous; seek shelter.
2–3Heavy rain or snowVisibility drops; muddy or snowy ground slows travel.
4–5Dense fogShort sightlines; navigation may be harder.
6–8Light rain or drizzleSlick ground; camp chores may take longer.
9–11Overcast and coolNo major effect; the day feels gloomy.
12–15Clear and mildIdeal travel conditions.
16–17Warm or hotWater and shade become more important.
18–19Strong windsSailing, flying, ranged attacks, or open fires may be affected.
20Magical weatherRoll on the d6 table below or invent an omen.

System-neutral homebrew. Adjust the effects to fit your edition, terrain, and campaign.
